STUDY PLAN

The Bachelor's degree in Video Games and Multimedia Applications focuses on a comprehensive training, allowing students to discover the main areas within game creation and multimedia applications. Discover more about the three levels of your degree's game that will help you find your own path and specialization.

LEVEL 1: TUTORIAL

The Beginning - Learn the theory and basic tools.

1st Semester - Level 1.1
Game Analysis and Criticism

You know how to play, but do you really know how to analyze a game?

History of Video Games

Discover the history of game development, decade by decade.

Introduction to Digital Imaging

Learn basic tools for image creation (even if you don't know how to draw!).

Communication Design

Understand concepts of design, color, balance, and readability (you want to make beautiful games, right?).

Foundations of Programming

Learn the basics and fundamentals of programming.

Creative Programming

Apply programming to concrete situations in a practical and creative way using Godot.

2nd Semester - Level 1.2
Screenplay and Interactive Narratives

Learn how to create non-linear stories and narratives.

Interaction Design

For others to understand your creations, you must learn the concepts of interaction and usability.

2D Digital Animation

Bring your characters to life by creating animation sets for games, either frame-by-frame or using bones.

Digital Illustration I

Learn to create backgrounds, characters, and sprites using various techniques.

Computer Graphics

Understand how computer graphics work and create your first 3D models.

Programming Languages I

Develop skills in programming and C# using Unity 3D.

LEVEL 2: MAIN QUEST

The Middle - Venture out and find your own path.

1st Semester - Level 2.1
Multimedia Applications Development

Discover the huge variety and potential of multimedia applications.

Digital Illustration II

Develop your own style and explore the creation of unique visual art styles.

Game Systems and Engines

Understand different systems and engines by creating a series of micro-games.

3D Modeling

Practice modeling and texturing props, environments, and characters.

Programming Languages II

Deepen your game programming knowledge.

Game Design Project I

Make a pitch, work in a team, and create your first 2D game.

2nd Semester - Level 2.2
Contemporary Media

Understand media in its various forms, from games to movies and even memes!

Multimedia and Web Production Studio

Explore all the potential and creativity of the web.

Introduction to Sound Design

Learn to create immersive sound environments.

3D Digital Animation

Bring your 3D models to life and animate your heroes and NPCs.

Programming Languages III

Develop complex systems and learn to use Unreal Engine.

Game Design Project II

Got a taste for making games? The challenge is even bigger now: build your 3D game.

LEVEL 3: FINAL BOSS

The End - You have leveled up your skills, it's time to face the final boss.

1st Semester - Level 3.1
Digital Arts Studio

Explore computer vision, sensors, and alternative ways to produce digital art.

Video Games and Multimedia Applications Production Studio I

Start your final course project.

Audiovisual Technologies

Learn audiovisual language to create games that are even more cinematic.

2nd Semester - Level 3.2
Video Games and Multimedia Applications Production Studio II

Finish the course with your most ambitious project.

Marketing and Advertising Studio

Create a marketing strategy and make everyone talk about your project.

Internship and Professional Integration

You are ready to face professional life, show what you are worth in your internship!
