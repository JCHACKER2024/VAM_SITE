CREATING THE FUTURE

VAM is a Bachelor's degree with an intense practical component that prepares you directly for the job market. Not sure if you would like to follow the path of programming, art, sound design, or game design? You are in the right place, as you will have the opportunity to learn and experiment in all of these fields, gaining a comprehensive vision of the main workflows and tasks in Video Games and Multimedia Applications development. The group projects we develop will allow you to specialize in the areas where you show the most skill; this way, each student's path is personalized, and distinct profiles are built within each class. The course's multiple modules are highly interconnected, making you feel like you are always working on smaller pieces of a larger project.

HIGHLIGHTED FEATURES

Skill

PITCH VAM

Do you find it hard to speak in public? We are here to help you overcome that! Work presentations are a key component of our course, and all students will learn how to pitch their ideas in the best possible way. Projects are chosen based on merit, creativity, and feasibility, and are voted on by both professors and students.

Community

DISCORD VAM

We have an active Discord community where students, alumni, professors, and industry professionals can interact online, showcase their work, ask questions, access job opportunities, and discuss games, movies, anime, geek culture, and more. It goes way beyond a simple WhatsApp group.

Annual Event

VAM GAME JAM

On the last weekend of January, Lusófona University opens its doors for 48 hours exclusively for VAM and friends. As an official JAM Site representing the global event Global Game JAM, we welcome everyone interested in building video games in a 48-hour marathon of collaboration, creativity, gaming, and pizza!

Exhibition

MOSTRA VAM

The culmination of each semester takes place during our public showcase of student-developed projects. It is the perfect environment to playtest and gather feedback from peers, professors, and industry experts. Attendees also vote to elect the best projects of the year across several categories. At times, we expand beyond the university walls, exhibiting at events like MEO XL GAMES or interactive art showcases.

CAREER OPPORTUNITIES

Video Game Studios

Whether in an Indie studio setup or inside a large team, locally or internationally, you will be fully prepared for the demands of the industry.

Serious Games Development

Gamification principles are vital for creating engaging content for advertising, simulation training, educational environments, or public awareness across any sector of society.

Digital Animation Studios

Apply your 2D and 3D animation knowledge to communicate ideas in highly creative, high-impact formats.

Web and Multimedia Content Creation & Management

Build custom websites and coordinate the complete content production pipeline for social media and interactive multimedia applications.

Audiovisual Art, Multimedia and AI

Blaze your own trail, explore new technologies, and shape the creative career paths and tech-driven roles of tomorrow.
