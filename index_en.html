BEYOND THE GAME!

The Bachelor's degree in Video Games and Multimedia Applications (VAM) at Universidade Lusófona Porto is organized into 3 levels, corresponding to the 3 years of the course.

We won the "Fazer +" Award for "Best Innovative Pedagogical Practice" in recognition of our pedagogical strategy that uses the Project Based Learning (PBL) methodology. This means you will learn while creating your own projects and developing your portfolio, following your own path in an exciting and motivating work process.

We play, analyze, and dream of the virtual worlds we will create together.
