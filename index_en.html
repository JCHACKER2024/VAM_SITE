BEYOND THE GAME!

The Bachelor's Degree in Video Games and Multimedia Applications (VAM) at Lusófona University Porto is organized into 3 levels corresponding to the 3 years of the course.

We won the Fazer + "Best Innovative Pedagogical Practice" Award in recognition of our pedagogical strategy that uses the Project Based Learning (PBL) methodology. This means you will learn while creating your own projects and developing your portfolio, following your own path in an exciting and motivating workflow.

We play, analyze, and dream of the virtual worlds that we will create together.
